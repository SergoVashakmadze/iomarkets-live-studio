Off air

Use headphones: the anchor's voice must not reach the microphone, or it counts as the guest talking. To check a chart, hold it up to the camera and press Show chart.

Fact-check

Figures the guest says or shows are checked here as the interview runs.

Segment

Each answer is cut out of its background by SAM 3.1 while the interview goes on. Clips are deleted 30 minutes after the interview ends: download what you need.

The anchor is an AI system and its voice is synthetic (Microsoft Edge text-to-speech). Guest speech is transcribed by Meta's Muse Voice Transcribe; the anchor, chart reading and fact-checks run on Muse Spark. Nothing here is investment advice. Built with Muse Spark Open.
